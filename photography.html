Hobby · Photography

The other lens
I look through.

When I'm not training models, I'm usually out with a camera — chasing the same thing either way: the moment a pattern resolves into something worth keeping. Below are a few of my favorite frames.

Photography is my hobby outside of work — the analog counterpart to everything I do with models during the day. Where ML is about finding signal in noise at scale, a camera is about finding it one frame at a time: the way light falls on a stairwell, the gap in a crowd that makes a street shot work.

I shoot mostly with a Fujifilm X100V and a couple of manual prime lenses — no flash, minimal editing. I'd rather wait for the light to be right than fix it afterward.
