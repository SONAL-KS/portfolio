About

Grounded in research, built for production.

01

I'm an AI/ML engineer with a background in applied NLP and distributed systems. My work sits at the point where a research idea has to survive contact with real traffic, real data drift, and real latency budgets.

Over the past six years I've shipped retrieval-augmented systems, fine-tuned LLMs, and computer vision pipelines for teams that needed more than a notebook — they needed something that could run unattended, get monitored, and get better over time.

I care most about the unglamorous half of ML: evaluation, observability, and the guardrails that let a model be trusted in production. I'm currently exploring agentic tool-use and small, efficient models fine-tuned for narrow, high-value tasks.
